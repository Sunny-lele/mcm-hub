
# MCM Hub

A curated archive of Mathematical Contest in Modeling (MCM) and Interdisciplinary Contest in Modeling (ICM) problems, modeling resources, and competition notes.

## About

This project organizes official MCM/ICM problems by year and problem type, making it easier to explore past contests and prepare for future competitions.

## Problem Categories

- **MCM A — Continuous Modeling**
- **MCM B — Discrete Modeling**

- **ICM D — Operations Research and Related Topics**
- **ICM E — Sustainability and Environmental Topics**
- **ICM F — Policy and Decision Making**

These labels are broad study categories. Specific problem topics vary by year.

## Goals

- Build a year-by-year archive of official contest problems.
- Record problem summaries, keywords, and modeling methods.
- Collect publicly available solution papers and learning resources.
- Document modeling experiments and competition preparation notes.
